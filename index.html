加油喵（Joyomo）隐私政策

生效日期 / Effective Date：2026 年 10 月 25 日

中文 English

1. 我们不收集任何数据

我们非常重视你的隐私。「加油喵（Joyomo）」（下称「本应用」）由个人开发者开发运营，不收集、不存储、不上传、不共享任何个人信息，包括但不限于：

  • 不收集你的姓名、电话、邮箱、位置、通讯录、照片等任何个人身份信息；
  • 不请求任何敏感系统权限（无相机、麦克风、定位、存储等权限）；
  • 不包含任何第三方统计、广告或分析 SDK；
  • 不连接任何开发者自建服务器，无网络数据上传。

2. 本地存储的数据

为了在下次打开时恢复你的设置，本应用仅将以下内容保存在你的设备本地：
  • 你输入的滚动文字；
  • 字号、字重、字体颜色、背景颜色、滚动速度、镜像开关、语言等偏好设置。
上述数据仅存储于设备本地的应用数据目录中，永远不会离开你的设备。卸载本应用后，这些数据将一并删除。

3. 儿童隐私

本应用不针对儿童收集任何数据（事实上本应用不收集任何人的数据），亦不包含任何不适合儿童的内容。

4. 第三方服务

本应用不包含广告、统计、推送或任何第三方数据服务。通过应用商店（Apple App Store / Google Play）购买时产生的交易数据由相应商店及其支付服务处理，本应用无法访问。

5. 政策变更

如本政策发生重大变更，我们将在应用更新说明或本页面中公告。变更后继续使用即视为接受更新后的政策。

6. 联系我们

如对本政策有任何疑问，请在本仓库提交 Issue 反馈。

1. We Collect No Data

Your privacy matters to us. "Joyomo (加油喵)" (the "App") is developed and operated by an individual developer and does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, including but not limited to:

  • No personal identity information (name, phone number, email, location, contacts, photos, etc.) is collected;
  • No sensitive system permissions are requested (no camera, microphone, location, or storage access);
  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included;
  • The App connects to no developer-operated servers and uploads no data over the network.

2. Data Stored Locally

To restore your settings on the next launch, the App stores the following only on your device:
  • The marquee text you type;
  • Your preferences (font size, weight, colors, scroll speed, mirror toggle, language, etc.).
These data never leave your device and are removed when you uninstall the App.

3. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children, and contains no content inappropriate for children.

4. Third-Party Services

The App contains no ads, analytics, or third-party data services. Purchases made through app stores (Apple App Store / Google Play) are processed by those stores and their payment providers; the App has no access to such transaction data.

5. Changes to This Policy

Material changes will be announced on this page or in the App's release notes. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue in this repository.
